Hi! I'm Xinyu (Ray) Chen, a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), advised by Prof. [Xiaoqi (Danny) Chen](https://engineering.purdue.edu/~xiaoqic/).

My research focuses on **computer systems and networking**. I work on making machine learning practical in high-speed networks, using programmable switches and DPUs to bring computation closer to the data. My current projects involve in-network machine learning, P4 and eBPF programming, and efficient systems for ML inference and code generation.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Statistics from the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) in May 2025. At UIUC, I worked with Prof. [Fan Lai](https://www.fanlai.me/) on Machine Learning for System, and with Prof. Klara Nahrstedt on wireless network scheduling.

I'm interested in systems that connect learning algorithms with the realities of hardware, networking, and deployment.
