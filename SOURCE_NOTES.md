# Sources and customization

This website adapts the MIT-licensed [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio) theme as configured in [Yifan Yu's website](https://github.com/yifan1130/yifan1130.github.io). The original MIT license is retained in `LICENSE`.

The About and CV pages are based on the owner's supplied academic background. Publication titles, author lists, venues, and DOIs were checked against publisher records and university publication pages. EMA's equal-contribution annotation is also listed on [Fan Lai's homepage](https://www.fanlai.me/).

Publication descriptions are short editorial summaries, not the papers' original abstracts. Publication thumbnails are conceptual illustrations, not figures or experimental results from the papers. The portrait is a temporary initials image.
